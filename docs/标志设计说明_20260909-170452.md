# Model Bridge 标志设计说明

- 生成方式：内置 ImageGen。
- 设计：以两段相连的桥形构成字母 M，表达多种模型的连接与统一入口；沿用项目青绿到蓝色的配色。
- 高清原图：`web/public/brand/model-bridge-logo-v1.png`，透明 PNG，1254 × 1254 像素。
- Google 上传版：`web/public/brand/model-bridge-google-logo-120-v1.png`，透明 PNG，120 × 120 像素。
- 网站显示版：`web/public/brand/model-bridge-mark-192-v1.png`，透明 PNG，192 × 192 像素，由共享组件 `web/src/components/BrandLogo.vue` 引用。
- 浏览器标签图标：`web/public/favicon.png`，32 × 32；主屏幕图标：`web/public/apple-touch-icon.png`，180 × 180。
- 各尺寸版本均由高清原图等比例导出，图案未更改。

## 网站替换范围

- Vue 网站：首页导航与页脚、统一登录页、旧用户登录视图、邀请页、管理员与用户侧边栏、API 文档页、充值弹窗。
- HTML 演示：共享品牌标志与关于页面、23 个 HTML 入口的 favicon 和主屏幕图标；资源随演示保存在 `html/assets/` 内。
- 移除两处旧 `favicon.svg`，引用改为新版 PNG 并增加版本查询参数，减少浏览器缓存旧图标的问题。
- 前端类型检查、生产构建、HTML 脚本语法检查通过。Chrome 验证主要页面、23 个 HTML 入口、手机端、深色模式及收起侧边栏；充值弹窗验证使用本地模拟接口，无真实交易。

Google 应用 Logo 支持 PNG，推荐正方形 120 × 120，文件不超过 1 MB。本次上传文件已按尺寸和格式检查。品牌显示仍由 Google 的品牌验证状态决定，图片规格检查不代表 Google 已审核通过。来源：[Google 官方品牌设置与 Logo 要求](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/15549049?hl=en-uk)。

## 完整生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished application logo for Model Bridge, an independent AI API gateway connecting multiple AI model providers. Intended for the website identity, browser/app icon, and Google OAuth consent branding.
Primary request: Create ONE original, exceptionally refined premium technology brand SYMBOL, with the quiet confidence and optical precision of a professional identity design studio. A compact architectural M formed from two connected bridge spans / folded continuous pathways: the idea of separate model streams becoming one coherent connection. Use sophisticated negative space to make the bridge connection legible without illustrative detail. The mark must have a distinctive memorable silhouette, well-proportioned geometric construction, consistent substantial ribbon width, subtly softened corners, and optical balance. Make the M/bridge gesture immediately elegant, not a generic chain link or infinity symbol.
Color palette: the established Model Bridge palette, rich turquoise/teal #00B7A2 flowing subtly toward clear medium blue #4F7CFF. Keep color transitions restrained and smooth. Ensure strong contrast on white, with no pale washed-out ends.
Style/medium: clean vector-like 2D logo artwork, crisp edges, premium minimalism, precise curves. The design itself is flat; no 3D effects.
Composition/framing: a SINGLE optically centered standalone icon on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background, square 1024 by 1024 canvas. Icon occupies approximately 74 percent of the canvas width with generous evenly balanced clear space. Keep a strong compact near-square silhouette that remains readable when reduced to 120 by 120 and 32 by 32 pixels.
Text: none. No wordmark, captions, slogan, or extra characters. The M is constructed as an abstract symbol, not typeset text.
Constraints: original independent brand identity. No Google G, Google multicolor palette, OpenAI knot, or recognizable third-party symbols. No surrounding app tile, no background panel, no visible transparency checkerboard, no presentation board, no variants, no small decorative nodes, no circuitry, no sparkles, no drop shadows, no bevel, no glass, no chrome, no glow, no textures, no watermark. Deliver the final clean transparent PNG logo asset, not a mockup.
```
