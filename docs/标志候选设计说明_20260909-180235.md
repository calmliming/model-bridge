## A｜桥形 MB 字母标：完整提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original premium symbol for Model Bridge, an independent AI API gateway connecting multiple model providers. This is a professional identity-design exploration, not a UI mockup.
Style: disciplined geometric construction, distinctive silhouette, confident substantial strokes, beautiful negative space, carefully tuned optical proportions, sharp antialiased edges with no stray pixels. Sophisticated 2D graphic design, not a 3D illustration. It must work at 32px and 120px.
Palette: the established Model Bridge turquoise/teal #00B7A2 and medium blue #4F7CFF, with strong contrast against white and dark backgrounds.
Composition: exactly ONE standalone symbol, optically centered in a square 1024x1024 transparent PNG. Approximately 74% canvas width and height, balanced generous margins. Genuine alpha transparency, no visible checkerboard.
No wordmark, captions, slogan, or extra text. No mockup, no presentation board, no multiple variants, no surrounding app tile, no drop shadow, no glow, no bevel, no metallic or glass effect, no texture, no tiny decorative nodes, no circuit-board details, no watermark. Original independent design: no Google G or multicolor Google treatment, no OpenAI knot, no recognizable third-party mark.
Design direction: a compact architectural MB monogram, elegantly fusing the initials M and B into a single custom-built geometric glyph. Think precision-cut modular bridge segments, shared vertical stems, a balanced M-shaped negative-space notch, and a subtly implied B in the right-hand pair of open counters. The letters should be integrated into one cohesive near-square silhouette, not two separate typeset letters. Use clean flat two-tone color blocks: blue as the primary structure and teal as a deliberate connecting segment. No gradient for this version. A feeling of clarity, engineering rigor, reliability and quiet luxury. Favor simplicity over clever complexity; generous open counters and a small number of robust shapes. This is the strongest minimal corporate direction.
```
